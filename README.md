# SigtGrid

**A free companion app for Valheim.** SigtGrid reads your Valheim character on your own PC and
turns it into stats, a play journal and a spoiler-free map of everything you've explored.

> Beta. Windows 10/11. Fan-made; not affiliated with or endorsed by Iron Gate or Coffee Stain.

## What it does

- **Stats and journal:** kills, deaths, skills, trophies, bosses, and a journal entry for every
  play session, with room for your own notes.
- **Spoiler-free map:** only what your character has explored, with terrain drawn from your
  world seed. Height contours, a surveyor's grid, three map styles.
- **Pins:** search, filter and tidy your map pins, rename or delete them in bulk, add new ones,
  and send the changes back to the game.
- **Planning:** routes, planned building areas, portal links, a crafting calculator for your
  group and a food planner.
- **Sign workshop:** coloured signs and multi-line pin names without writing the codes by hand.
- **Optional live mode:** with the small SigtGridLink plugin you see yourself move on the map and
  can send pins to the running game.
- **Built-in guide** (F1) explaining every feature.

## Download and install

1. Download **`SigtGrid Setup <version>.exe`** from the latest release on the right.
2. Run it. If Windows says *"Windows protected your PC"*, click **More info → Run anyway**.
   The app isn't code-signed yet; that's all the warning means.
3. SigtGrid finds your characters by itself, local and Steam Cloud.

SigtGrid **updates itself**: new versions download in the background, and you choose when to
restart.

## Your save files

- SigtGrid only reads your files, except when you press **Send to Valheim** to write pin changes.
  It always makes a backup first (Settings → Backups, with Restore).
- **Steam Cloud:** if Steam reports a cloud conflict after SigtGrid changed a character, choose the
  **local** files.
- It's a beta: keeping your own copy of your character now and then is a good idea.

## Live mode (optional)

Needs BepInEx for Valheim (easiest with r2modman). Install the plugin from
**Settings → Live position on the map**, then start Valheim with BepInEx. SigtGridLink is a
client-side mod that only touches your own map pins; check that your server allows mods.

## Privacy

Everything stays on your PC. No account, no tracking. The only connection SigtGrid makes is
checking this page for new versions (can be turned off in Settings).
